**Words to Know Part 3** Emancipate: to set someone free from slavery. Greenbacks: paper money used by the United States. Goobers: peanuts. Hard Knocks: difficult experiences or hard lessons in life. Hunkey Dorey: everything is fine or okay. Skedaddle: to run away quickly. --- **Station 7: 54th Massachusetts Infantry** 1. Frederick Douglass helped recruit African Americans to the army. 2. Robert Gould Shaw was chosen to lead the 54th unit. He was chosen because he was a white officer from a respected family and supported the cause. 3. They made their famous stand at Fort Wagner near Charleston. About 40 percent were casualties. 4. About 180,000 African Americans joined the war effort in the North. 5. William Harvey Carney became the first African American awarded the Medal of Honor. 6. The memorial is located in Boston Common. --- **Station 8: Daily Life of a Soldier and Desertion** 1. Uniforms were usually made of wool. Union uniforms were blue. 2. Many soldiers slept on the ground because tents were crowded, heavy, or not always available. 3. Soldiers played cards, wrote letters, sang songs, read, told stories, and played games. 4. Confederate uniforms were usually gray or butternut. Soldiers were often missing shoes, blankets, food, and proper clothing. 5. They were similar because both sides had hard lives, poor food, disease, and long marches. They were different because Union soldiers usually had better supplies, while Confederate soldiers often lacked supplies. 6. The average pay of a Union soldier was about $13 a month. African American soldiers were first paid about $10 a month, with $3 taken out for clothing. 7. Many people deserted because army life was dangerous, food was bad, pay was low, and they missed their families.
